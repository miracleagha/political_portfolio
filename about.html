Hon. Blessing U. Benyeogor
Public Leadership Community Advocate

A Voice For Every Community

Blessing Ufedojo Benyeogor is a passionate advocate for inclusive development, youth participation, women empowerment, and accountable leadership. She believes the FCT deserves responsive governance, stronger institutions, safer communities, and greater opportunities for every resident.

Her vision is simple: to build a Federal Capital Territory where government works for the people and every community has the opportunity to thrive. She listens first, learns continuously, and leads with the clarity that service — not status — is the measure of real leadership.
