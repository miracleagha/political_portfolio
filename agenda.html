Our Priorities

Our Agenda For A Better FCT

Practical priorities for a safer, more inclusive, and more prosperous Federal Capital Territory. The eleven focus areas below guide every engagement, every proposal, and every community conversation.

01 Governance 02 Security 03 Housing 04 Education 05 Healthcare 06 Emergency 07 Welfare 08 Drainage 09 Youth 10 Women 11 Electricity
01

Transparent & Accountable Governance

Promoting openness, responsibility, and accountability in public service — because communities deserve to know how decisions are made and how resources are used.

02

Stronger Security

Supporting safer communities through better security coordination, improved response, and partnerships that keep every neighbourhood and family safer.

03

Affordable Housing

Advocating accessible housing opportunities for families and young professionals — so where you live is not a barrier to building a life.

04

Quality Education

Improving educational infrastructure and learning opportunities — in classrooms, communities, and the digital tools that prepare young minds for the future.

05

Accessible Healthcare

Promoting affordable and quality healthcare services, closer to where residents live, with trained personnel and reliable supply systems.

06

Emergency Medical Response

Supporting ambulance services and rapid emergency response systems so that in the critical first minutes, help is already on the way.

07

Teachers, Doctors & Nurses Welfare

Championing better welfare and working conditions for the professionals who hold up our schools, clinics and hospitals every single day.

08

Flood Control & Drainage

Addressing flooding through modern drainage systems and infrastructure — because rains should not routinely wash away homes, livelihoods or lives.

09

Youth Empowerment

Creating opportunities for entrepreneurship, employment, and leadership — because young people are already building the future we want.

10

Women Empowerment

Supporting women through education, business, and leadership opportunities — because when women succeed, families and communities prosper.

11

Reliable Electricity

Advocating practical energy solutions and improved power supply — the quiet foundation under every job, every classroom, and every business.

Note on claims: This agenda represents our focus and advocacy priorities. Specific funding allocations, implementation dates, legislative outcomes and verified achievements will only be published after the campaign team confirms them. We will not present proposals as outcomes.
